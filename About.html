Visionary Leadership

Priscilla Mabhena

Managing Director & Founder

Priscilla Mabhena is an award-winning business leader and the driving strategic force behind Cilla's Conference Centre. With a deep commitment to elevating corporate hosting standards and bespoke private events, she has built the venue into a premier destination recognized for excellence.

Her approach combines precise executive event management with personalized, high-end hospitality. Under her leadership, the center continues to design and execute seamless corporate seminars, milestone private celebrations, and top-tier culinary catering experiences that leave lasting impressions.

"We don't just host events; we provide the pristine environments where strategic leadership connects and beautiful milestones are celebrated."
